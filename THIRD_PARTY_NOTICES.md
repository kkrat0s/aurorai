# Third-party sources and notices

- **NASA CCMC CME Scoreboard / NASA SPDF OMNI:** scientific records and attribution remain NASA/provider material. These are linked and bounded according to the [rights manifest](docs/provenance-rights.md); agency marks and unrelated third-party images are not licensed by this repository.
- **NOAA SWPC OVATION / RTSW:** modeled activity and upstream measurements retain NOAA attribution, original timestamps and source SHA-256 manifests. Model activity is not empirical local visibility probability.
- **USGS BOU:** adjusted historical observatory records are credited to USGS. Unknown operational availability is retained.
- **Broomfield archive:** Zenodo record 18928938, provider metadata CC BY 4.0. See the exact author/title/license metadata in the inspection report. Raw private-location files are excluded from distribution. Repeated timestamps and missing sensor metadata constrain scientific use.
- **Solar geometry:** original implementation of approximate NOAA fractional-year equations from https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/solareqns.PDF. Approximate geometric altitude only; no atmospheric refraction/weather/terrain correction.
- **Next.js, React, Expo, React Native, Zod, TypeScript, Vitest, ESLint:** packages and their dependencies retain package-provided licenses. Exact installed versions are in the root and mobile lockfiles. Do not remove their license notices when redistributing bundles.
- **Python scientific packages:** NumPy, pandas, SciPy and scikit-learn retain their BSD-family package licenses. Versions are pinned in `science/requirements.txt`.
- **Approved wireframe:** user-supplied, AI-generated conceptual design reference. Its displayed scientific readings are fictional and are not reused as measurements.

No third-party aurora photographs, basemap tiles, external fonts, unlicensed image archive or exact volunteer location pins are bundled in the web interface. The polar map is a geographic graticule and provider data cells, not a detailed land basemap.
