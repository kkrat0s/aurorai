# Source provenance and rights

Every demo numeric series comes from a named public scientific source or is explicitly labeled as a mathematical/synthetic test. The approved conceptual wireframe is a design reference, never a source of measurements.

| Source | Acquired content | Rights and attribution | Public artifact policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| NASA CCMC CME Scoreboard, SW-01/02 | May 2024 numeric predictions, method names, submission times, observed arrival | [Public API and Scoreboard terms](https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/scoreboards/cme/earth/); credit NASA CCMC and named submitting teams. CCMC requests notification before event/validation studies and presentations. | Small factual subset only; full third-party prose/notes excluded. No license to all community materials is presumed. Public presentation/outreach review still open; no messages sent. |
| USGS BOU, GR-01/02 | Adjusted XYZF one-minute values and scientific metadata | [USGS-produced data are U.S. public domain](https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits); credit U.S. Geological Survey. | Attributed X/F subset and numerical derived learning cases; no agency logos or endorsement. |
| NOAA SWPC, SW-08/09 | Current OVATION and active-source RTSW samples | [NOAA data-product reuse guidance](https://www.noaa.gov/office-education/outreach-communication/faq); credit NOAA SWPC; no endorsement. | Small timestamped factual subsets. Current frame is not an issued 2024 map. |
| NASA OMNI HRO five-minute, SW-04/05 | Fixed event windows of IMF GSM/GSE and plasma | [OMNI acknowledgment guidance](https://omniweb.gsfc.nasa.gov/html/ow_min_data.html); credit NASA GSFC/SPDF OMNIWeb and contributing instrument teams. | Physical AuroraSense phone sessions, PH-11 | Two owner-provided iPhone raw/QC pairs; canonical QC verified: partial interference run (3,042 samples / 304.844 s) and full stationary attempt (5,987 / 600.032 s), both scientifically rejected by unchanged QC | Exports include adult/local-recording consent; private retention preserved. Independent hardware calibration/provenance is unverified. | Raw exports, detailed QC and plots remain ignored/private. Only aggregate measured results and hashes appear in the public report. No empirical-noise coefficient or network simulation is inferred from the unstable baselines. |

Exact response SHA-256, retrieval UTC, source URL, byte size and ETag where supplied are in `demo/fixtures/verified-sources.json`, the science result manifests, and private raw-response sidecars. The Broomfield public report includes verified MD5/SHA-256 and license-metadata SHA-256 without location-bearing bytes. Historical professional data carry null operational availability. Provider-submitted CME issuance times have their basis described explicitly. OVATION issuance is unknown; its observation and forecast-valid times are preserved independently.

All original raw downloads are ignored, even where redistribution would be allowed. Credentials, volunteer locations and unlicensed image archives must never be included in the public package. The only Broomfield numerical output redistributed is original aggregate analysis under the cited CC BY 4.0 source attribution; the source has not been certified as scientifically suitable.

No cloud accounts were modified and no external researcher or provider was contacted. CCMC presentation notification and final attribution checks are owner actions before public submission; local implementation and read-only inspection proceed now.
